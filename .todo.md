## 5. Privacy / analytics

- [ ] **GA + GTM double counting.** The site loads Google Analytics directly
      AND Google Tag Manager. If the GTM container (GTM-WTCF7TTR) also has a
      GA4 tag, every visit is counted twice. Check in GTM and keep only one.
      The loader is `window.loadAnalytics` in `index.html`.
- [ ] **Privacy notice.** Swiss nFADP / EU GDPR expect a short privacy page
      that says what's collected (GA cookies) and how to contact you. Add a
      page or a section, and link it from the cookie banner
      (`src/CookieConsent.jsx`).
- [ ] **Self-host the Google Fonts** (Playfair Display, DM Sans). They're
      loaded with an `@import` at the top of `src/App.css`, which slows the
      first render and sends visitor IPs to Google (German courts have fined
      sites for this). Use `@fontsource/playfair-display` and
      `@fontsource/dm-sans` from npm instead.
